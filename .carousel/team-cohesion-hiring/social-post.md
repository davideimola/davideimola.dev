At RedCarbon we bring AI to cybersecurity. After this week, I'm starting to think the next frontier is sailing. Or golf. A bit of fine-tuning and a few thousand euros of GPU time, and surely we could train a model that turns us pro.

Anyway. Jokes aside.

We're a remote team, so most of our days happen through a screen. Earlier this week a group of us spent one of those days together on Lake Garda instead. Our CEO, Federico, put it together in about a week, for whoever lived close enough to the lake. No agenda, no team-building consultant, no keynote. We're not strangers online, and we do meet up now and then (we ran a hackathon about a month ago). But a day like this does something for a team that a normal working week just can't.

I loved it more than I expected to. On the engineering side we finally got to hash things out in person, sketch new ideas out loud, argue about the stuff we usually just type at each other. Federico was the only non-technical one in the group, and honestly talking to him was one of the best parts: hearing how we're actually doing, how the market sees us, where we're placing our bets. From behind a monitor you lose sight of all of that.

Then we actually played golf. Most of us had never held a club in our lives (one colleague clearly had, and it showed). No real course, just the driving range, a sand dune to clear, a few balls toward a hole on an easy stretch. Being equally bad at something together does more for a team than any icebreaker I've ever sat through.

The sailboat is the part I keep coming back to. Almost everyone took a turn steering, and getting the thing to move actually took cooperation: pulling on the sail, trimming, listening to each other. Then the wind did the rest and we drifted across the lake with no engine noise, rocked by the water. It's hard to explain how much your head clears out there.

Here's what stuck with me: you don't need a huge, over-planned offsite for any of this. One simple thing, thrown together in a week, was enough to recharge and look at what's next with a lot more optimism.

We're growing the engineering team at RedCarbon. Two roles I'd love to fill right now: a Senior Software Engineer (bonus points if you're strong on the frontend) and an AI Engineer. If a team that closes the laptop to go be bad at golf together sounds like your kind of place, DM me and I'll tell you more.
