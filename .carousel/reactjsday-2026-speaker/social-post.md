I'm honestly fired up about this one. A talk getting picked always feels good, but this is a stage well outside my comfort zone, and that's exactly why I wanted it. Backend person, React conference, design and AI. If that sounds like an odd fit, good. That's the challenge.

Here's the thing: the talk isn't really about React. It's about what quietly falls apart when you hand your frontend to AI and hope for the best.

The title is "AI Won't Design Your System. You Have To.", and it comes from something I learned the hard way while rebuilding my own site. Twice.

The first time I let AI drive. Every component looked fine on its own, but put together it was a mess: five shades of almost-the-same red, spacing that never quite repeated, buttons that each had their own opinion. The problem wasn't the AI. It was me, because I never gave it a system to build inside.

So I started over. I defined the design system first, the tokens, the type scale, the actual rules, and only then let AI touch the components. Same tool, completely different result.

And this didn't stay a personal-site experiment. We brought the same idea into our work at the company, thanks to Elena, Gabriele, and Ludovico. I'll walk through all of it live, with Claude Code, on the real thing that broke.
